# Peyton Pours — Business Cards (No Phone) · VistaPrint Upload Guide

## Files
Each is 1125 × 675 px = **3.75″ × 2.25″ at 300 DPI** (3.5″ × 2″ trim + 0.125″ bleed all around).

- `light-front-300dpi.png` — cream front, martini + wordmark
- `light-back-300dpi.png` — pink back, contact info
- `dark-front-300dpi.png` — charcoal front, martini + wordmark
- `dark-back-300dpi.png` — blush back, dark-pink contact info

Pair front + back from the **same theme**. Recommended: Light front + Light back, or Dark front + Dark back.

## VistaPrint steps
1. Standard Business Cards → size **3.5″ × 2″**, orientation **Horizontal**.
2. Choose **"Upload your own design"** (not the template editor) → **Two-sided**.
3. Upload the front file, then the back file.
4. When VistaPrint shows the bleed/safety preview, drag the image so it **fills the full bleed box** — edges should extend past the dashed trim line. Don't scale it down to fit inside the trim line.
5. Confirm the low-res warning does *not* appear (these are 300 DPI, so it shouldn't).

## Stock recommendation
- **Premium Matte** or **Ultra-Thick Matte** — the cream and blush read cleanest matte; glossy will shift the pink warmer and show fingerprints on the charcoal.
- Avoid glossy/UV for the dark theme.

## Safe-area note
All text sits well inside the 0.125″ safety margin, so minor trim variance won't clip anything.
